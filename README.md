## 📱 Essential Apps

### Communication
- **WeChat** — Messaging, payments, and mini-programs (essential for China)
- **QQ** — Alternative messaging popular in China

### Navigation
- **Baidu Maps** — Best maps for China (Chinese interface)
- **Amap (Gaode)** — Alternative maps with good English support
- **Didi** — Chinese ride-hailing app

### Travel Booking
- **Trip.com (Ctrip)** — Flights, hotels, trains in English
- **Qunar** — Price comparison for travel
- **12306** — Official train ticket app

### Payments
- **Alipay** — Mobile payment, now supports international cards
- **WeChat Pay** — Integrated with WeChat, international cards supported

## 🔓 VPN & Internet Access

### Websites Blocked in China
- Google services (Search, Maps, Gmail, Translate)
- Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp
- Twitter/X, YouTube
- Reddit, Quora
- Most Western news sites

### Tools
- **[China Block Checker](https://aihubai.cn/block-checker.html)** — Check if a website works in China
- VPN services (research current options before traveling)
- International roaming data as alternative

## 💡 Travel Tips

1. **Translation**: Bookmark [ChinaPal AI](https://aihubai.cn) for instant translation without VPN
2. **Payments**: Set up Alipay/WeChat Pay with an international card before arrival
3. **Internet**: Get a VPN or local SIM card
4. **Transportation**: Book trains through Trip.com; use Didi for rides
5. **Cash**: Always carry some cash as backup
6. **VPN**: Install before arriving in China
7. **Registration**: Foreigners must register at local police station within 24 hours of arrival

## 🚨 Emergency Information

- **Police**: 110
- **Ambulance**: 120
- **Fire**: 119
- **Tourist Hotline**: 12301
- **General Hotline**: 12345 (English available in major cities)
